## Visitors to the public job pages (no account)

Browsing `/jobs`, the landing pages and the job pages sets **no cookies** and stores nothing about you. The server's
web log keeps the network address, time, page and browser name of each request for [N] days to keep the service
secure and working (legitimate interest). Fonts, styles and scripts all come from JobDesk's own server: no third
party is contacted when you open a page.

**Adverts:** there are none yet. If adverts are added (Google AdSense), they will be shown only after you choose in
a consent banner from a Google-certified consent platform; without consent only non-personalised adverts, or none,
are shown. This section will then list the advertising partners and cookies. [Fill in when AdSense is switched on.]

## Members (with an account)

As in the pilot notice: name and email for signing in with a one-time link, settings (fields, Finnish level, AI
choice), CVs, fit comparisons, tracked applications and saved jobs are stored for the account and visible only to the
member. Job ads a member shares are visible to all members, without the member's name. A strictly necessary session
cookie keeps you signed in. AI processing happens only as described there, and CV facts are sent only with the
member's "AI with your CV" switch on. [Copy the final tables from the pilot notice once it is approved.]

## Employers who post a job

The **Post a job** form stores the ad and the contact name and email address you give. The ad is published only
after an administrator approves it; the contact details are **never published**, are seen only by administrators, and
are used only to contact you about that ad. They are deleted automatically **90 days after the ad has been approved
or rejected**. The published ad stays listed until its deadline (or 60 days without one), and is then closed.
Legal basis: [legitimate interest in running the service / the employer's request to publish the ad].

## Job ads from public feeds

Ads from public job feeds (Valtiolle, Kuntarekry and others) are shown with their source and a link to the original.
They may contain a recruiter's name and phone number; **these are never shown on the public pages**, and are kept
only as long as the ad is listed. [Confirm the retention of closed ads: currently kept for members who track them.]
